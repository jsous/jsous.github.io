John Sous

Yale University

I am a theoretical physicist and an Assistant Professor of Applied Physics and Physics at Yale University. My group uses the tools of physics, such as statistical mechanics and information theory, to study condensed matter, quantum science, and artificial intelligence. We aim for a unifying understanding of interacting statistical systems, and for mechanistic explanations in specific problems of broad interest: the origin of scaling laws in modern AI systems, for example, or the physical mechanisms that could produce high-temperature superconductivity in real materials.
